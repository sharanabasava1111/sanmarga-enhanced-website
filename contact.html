Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find clear answers about Sanmarga Geleyara Balaga, free eye-care camps, school programmes, and volunteering.

Sanmarga Geleyara Balaga is a registered voluntary non-profit social organization founded in 2015 in Ballari, Karnataka. Built by people committed to societal duty, the Balaga focuses on four key pillars: free eye-care and health check-ups, educational mentoring and SSLC exam awareness, environmental protection (tree plantations & water conservation), and emergency community relief.

It is simple! Click the 'Open Enquiry / Contact Form' button on this page, choose 'Request a Free Health / Eye Camp' as your reason, and provide your village or institution name with your contact number. Our team will review the request and get in touch to schedule dates, coordinate volunteer doctors, and organize the event.

Yes, 100% free. Sanmarga Geleyara Balaga never charges any fees from beneficiaries. All consultations, vision tests, and distributed spectacles or essential medicines during our camps are provided completely free of charge to help underserved communities.

Every academic year, our volunteers visit government high schools (in Sandur, Ballari, and nearby areas) to conduct special orientation sessions for 10th standard SSLC students. These sessions focus on time management, memory retention, overcoming exam fear, and motivation to help rural students write their board exams with confidence.

Anyone with a heart for community service is warmly welcome! You can click the 'Open Enquiry / Contact Form' button, select 'Volunteer or Join the Balaga', and tell us about your skills and availability. You can also explore our Work With Us page or message us on Instagram.

If your locality faces summer drinking water issues, waste dumping, or lack of green cover, open the popup form and choose 'Report a Public / Community Problem'. Describe the location and specific problem. Our team will review the concern and coordinate a voluntary action or awareness drive.

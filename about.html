About us

Built by people who want to give back.

Sanmarga Geleyara Balaga, Ballari, began in 2015 with one idea: every person has a duty towards society. Since then, volunteers have worked in education, health and the environment.

We believe children should become more than successful: they should grow into compassionate doctors, engineers, teachers, farmers and public officers.

Vision

Sanmarga Geleyara Balaga envisions a united, empowered, and sustainable Ballari.

We dream of a society where every person has the opportunity to learn, stay healthy, and live in a clean and safe environment. Built on equality, dignity, and compassion, this is a Ballari where communities grow together and no one is left behind.

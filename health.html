Our health work

Dignity begins with accessible care.

Health camps are organised with qualified professionals and partner institutions to reach people who may otherwise postpone care.

  • Free eye screening and referral support
  • Community health check-up camps
  • Coordination with medical professionals and hospitals
  • Health information shared in clear, accessible language
